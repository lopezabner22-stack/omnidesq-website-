The Hidden Cost of Answering Your Own Phone

Every time your phone rings, you face an impossible choice. Answer it and interrupt the important work you're doing, or let it go to voicemail and risk losing a potential customer. For small business owners in Milwaukee and beyond, this constant juggling act eats away at productivity, focus, and ultimately, profitability. Research shows it takes an average of 23 minutes to fully regain focus after an interruption. Multiply that by a dozen calls per day, and you've lost hours of productive time.

The traditional solution has been hiring a receptionist, but that comes with its own challenges. Salary, benefits, training, coverage during sick days and vacations. For many small businesses, it's simply not feasible. Yet the phone still rings, and customers still expect professional, immediate service. This is where intelligent automation steps in, not to replace the human touch, but to amplify your ability to serve customers while protecting your time.
