What's inside

Every tool below is part of the same app and shares the same customer list, so a caller, a booking and a review all land on one person’s card.

Phone receptionist

An AI receptionist answers your calls day and night, in English or Spanish. It books appointments on your calendar, answers common questions, and adds the caller to your customer list.

Every business gets its own receptionist, with its own name and voice. Your number stays the same: you forward the calls you can’t pick up.

Online booking

A booking page with your services, prices, staff and hours. You can ask questions before someone books, and take a deposit that goes to your own Stripe account.

Bookings from the phone, the page and the app all land on one calendar, with a copy in your Google Calendar.

Customers

One card per person, with their calls, texts, appointments and notes in one place.

Moving from another system? Bring your contacts and appointments over from a spreadsheet export.

Texts

Booking confirmations and appointment reminders go out by text on their own, so fewer people forget to show up.

Posts and blog

A month of posts for Facebook, Instagram and your Google Business listing, plus blog articles, built from your own photos and videos and following your rules for what goes out and when.

You see the calendar and approve it before anything is published.

Ads

Your Google and Facebook ads are checked against a list of known problems, with fixes you can apply in one tap. Spending limits are set up front, and every change is logged and can be undone.

Reviews

When a new Google review comes in, you get a notification that opens on that review, with three suggested replies or room to write your own.

Website chat

A chat box for your website that answers visitors’ questions and takes booking requests, in English or Spanish.

Aria, the assistant inside the app

You don’t have to learn where everything is. Tell Aria what you need in plain words and she does it in the app: adds a customer, moves a post, shows you what went out today. She also walks you through setup one step at a time.

Getting started

We set it up with you. Most of it is connecting accounts you already have.

  1. Connect your accounts

    Sign in with Google and Facebook, and your website if you have one. That is how Omnidesq reaches your listing, your pages and your calendar.

  2. Share your photos and videos

    Point Aria to a Google Drive folder with your photos and videos. Your posts are built from your real work, not stock pictures.

  3. Approve and go

    We set up your booking page, your phone receptionist and your first month of posts. You look them over and approve them before they go live.

Your first month of the phone receptionist is free

Try it on your real calls. At the end of the month you see how many calls it answered and how many appointments it booked, and you decide whether to keep it. For the rest of the platform we give you a price based on what your business needs.
